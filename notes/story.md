# session 2

Dulce forgot something and ran home, will meet up with us again shortly. As we leave, a young emo with a ghost runs up to join us. Vale was not registered to join the challenge and we let him tag along (with concerns from Adeline).

We head down Route 1. The tall grass is uneventful for us, but afterward we pass two kids talking excitedly about Annemarie and Syrah Sauvignon. Adeline realizes they're playing the story of the ousting. She tries to teach them empathy. We beat them, feel bad and give them some cash for candy, and continue on!

We reach Millville, leveling up to 2.

# session 3

Dulce catches up, Adeline ducks out.

We hit the Pokemon Center and ask about the town.
- Enora, Poison-type gym leader
- the Guest boat parts factory tours
- Pokemart

We are effectively broke.  Ways of making money:
- ante'd trainer fights / gambling
- beating gyms
- asking around and helping people out
- larceny

We head to the factory and chat with the other team on the way in.  Reaffirm our hate for Brad and annoyance with Pippin and Velma, and try to teach Annemarie about living.

Tall older man, round cheeks, quintessential grandfather guy Graham speaking to Marty, a new guy nervously holding pen and clipboard who's shadowing Graham. We introduce ourselves and start the tour.

(We learn that Poison-type pokemon feed off emissions at the factory and release them in the gym battles, which in turn generates energy for the factory.  Pinot Guest-Sauvignon led these scientific advances while her wife Noir Sauvignon used political connections to support maritime infrastructure in the region.)

Lights flicker, loud crashes from below, Graham leaves us to check things out. Marty heads down, checking in with his Varoom Engie and we sneak after.  We reveal ourselves to help him with Graham's password reset questions.

Two rogue Eelektriks are playing in the middle of the room and wreaking havoc with the electrical systems.  We fight them and save the factory! This room is trashed though.  Marty finds a business card with **a toppled wine glass on it spilling black that shines red.**  Maybe the Pokemon were placed here by someone?

We leave and heal up. (Marty gives us the card.)

# session 4

Adeline drops out (police brutality? hating Pippin?)

We walk around town looking for things matching the symbol on the card we got from the factory. Doesn't seem to be a business. We eventually find it on a newspaper, front page news: a recent break-in at the Sauvignon family's island home on the day we left. No valuables stolen, but attempts were made at ingress to Noir Sauvignon's office. The same card was found there. We decide to talk to Annemarie.

We go to the gym, where our rivals are leaving victorious. We ask Annemarie about the break-in. It happened right after she left and she says she doesn't know what it means. When we show her the other card she says that it's Syrah's personal symbol, and that fact feels much more significant given its appearance in another location.

Barley has heard of a group of Syrah supporters meeting in Witchhaven. Annemarie hadn't really considered that Syrah might have beef with her even though she's been "rebelling" lately. We warn her and her team that they may be being followed and to watch out and stay safe.

We enter the gym, assert that Vale is Adeline, and enter the challenge.

- 5 baby pokemon and 5 identical dog beds

After investigating we get the order in 2 tries, earning a prize! We get a super potion (Greater Healing) from Graham and continue to the gym leader unhindered. We defeat Enora's team without much difficulty, gain our first gym badge, and level up! The badge is a smokestack with a purple blob of poison emerging from it.
